Sam Kowalski  10:22 AM
I own the booking service now, so I went and read it. pasting my notes from the code, not the code itself:

Sam Kowalski  10:23 AM
Booking#rebook!:
- sets the old booking row's status to REBOOKED
- inserts a NEW booking row with a new ref, rebooked_from_id pointing at the old row
- copies lane, dates, customer, commodity; the carrier assignment is cleared
- the invoice job treats the new row as a separate billable booking
Booking#amend!:
- updates the date fields in place, status AMENDED, same row, same ref
- does not call the carrier portal sync (that's the bug Maya means)
the customer portal shows the ref of the ORIGINAL booking for both, it follows rebooked_from_id back

Tom Brennan  10:25 AM
