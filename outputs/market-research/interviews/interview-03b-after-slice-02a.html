Q5When did you notice the line breaks were gone?

After Send. My message showed up in the log as one enormous paragraph: “Customer A booked 10:14 ops creates it on submit Tom 10:16 no finance only counts it when…”. Q6Then what did you do?
PR

The box was empty. My text was only in the log, as that wall, so I went back to the text file instead. I didn't know the limit, so I guessed. I deleted the first forty-odd messages, the “following” and “+1” and the original question, and kept the argument. It came to about seven thousand. That was another ten minutes. I pasted that and it went through. The reply came back in a couple of seconds.

Q7What did the reply say?
PR

A tidy summary: “The thread discusses differing definitions of when a booking is created,” then some advice to “align on a shared booking lifecycle and document status transitions.” No question for me at all. It never mentioned REBOOKED versus AMENDED, because the part where ops explains it was in what I'd cut. And it said ops thinks a booking starts when it's invoiceable. That's Tom's line, and Tom is finance. With the line breaks gone it glued Tom's sentence onto the ops person before him, so the one thing it attributed, it got wrong. That's the wrong-label problem I told you about last time, except this time it's real, not a mockup.

Q8How long did that take all together? What did you do next?

About forty minutes, with the sanitizing. Then I did what I always do. I pasted the same thread, with its line breaks, into our company Claude, which is allowed because it's sanitized anyway. decision #34
Q22One option on the table: a field where you paste your own Anthropic API key, so your text goes to Anthropic under your company's terms. Is that acceptable?
PR

For me, it doesn't matter, because I can't get one. Our Claude Enterprise is the chat workspace. There's no API key in it for me to copy. API access is a separate account the platform team runs.

Q23How do you know that? Has anyone on your team tried?
PR

Ravi, in July. He wanted to run a script that summarized Jira tickets. He filed a ticket with platform, had to name the service and an owner, and the key went into Vault. He's never seen the key itself. The script reads it at runtime. Nobody at my company holds a personal Anthropic key, and if they did, they wouldn't be allowed to paste it into a website.

Q24Say you had one. What would security say about pasting it into a third-party web app?
PR

Our secrets policy is one line: credentials never go into third-party sites. A key is worse than the thread. If your page leaks it, anyone can spend on our bill, and anything sent with it goes out under our contract. Then they'd ask the question I'd ask: does the key go to your server? If it does, you're still a vendor, just one that's holding our key now. If it goes straight from my browser to Anthropic, that's better, but they'd still want to read the code, and the code is on your site, not in our repo.

Q25Would a key field break “no settings before the first reply” for you?
PR

If it's optional and folded away, like “Use your own Anthropic key,” I'd ignore it, and it wouldn't bother me. If it's required, it's the account gate with a different name, and I'd bounce on the first visit. But the bigger point is: an optional field I can't fill in doesn't build anything for me. It's for somebody else. A consultant with a personal Anthropic account, maybe.

Q26What about self-hosting: the code as a repo you run locally or inside your company, pointed at your company's key?
PR

That's the only version that could be legal for real material. If it runs on my laptop or in our infra and calls Anthropic with a key from Vault, it's an internal tool, not a vendor. Same path as Ravi's script: a platform ticket and a code review. That took him about two weeks. But I'm not doing that for a tool I haven't had one win with, and not four weeks before an RFC. Self-hosting is what I'd do after it's already saved me a week, not to find out whether it can.

Q27So if we had to pick one: key field, self-host, or say plainly “this is for sanitized text”?
PR

For this month, say it plainly. I'm already sanitizing. Tell me the tool is built for that, tell me what to strip, and fill in the rest of the notice. Self-host is a later conversation, and it only happens if the sanitized version wins first. The key field I'd skip. It costs you a setting and it doesn't unlock me.
