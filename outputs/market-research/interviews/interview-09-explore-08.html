Roadmap hotspots

Assumptions this interview challenges

A “≠” linked to a row is self-explaining

#109 · #100

Keep the ≠ inside the term and the two quotes under the question. Style it as an open question, not an alert, and rename “not kept”. “Differs from” must quote a clause. Add a #100 gate case where the new line agrees with the linked row's words but breaks its unstated scope: the expected output is “row doesn't cover X”.

A count of settled rows builds trust

#109 · #90

Only if it's right and it opens. Fix the count (retired rows are not settled) and add an acceptance check that the count equals the settled chips. Each settled row shows “holds · <line>” or “not in this thread”.

“Settled” needs no owner

#90 · M1

A Slack line isn't a settlement. “Settled by <owner>, <date>”, as Marcus asked: doubt labels are credible when owned and dated.

Raising drift at the review is the natural default

#90

Add “Ask the owner first”. “Update the kept row” requires a source and strikes the old row. “Not a change” is remembered and says so.

Desk names are solved once #77 lands

#77

“Current practice” and “Dana's sheet” are new desk-hiding labels. Group names must be desk names in the kept glossary too, in their own words (“nights”).

Keeping the glossary in the browser is the moat
