Two concepts, two notices. 01b says “Not an approved vendor for you? Q23s3, “This conversation is full.” Would you trust the editable carried-over summary, or copy everything out first?
PR

Copy first, every time. In September my Booking notes chat in Claude hit the limit, and I asked it to summarize everything for a new chat. The summary merged REBOOKED and AMENDED into “rebook/amend” and dropped TONU. I found out on the 29th, when Dana said TONU and my summary didn’t have it. Since then I paste the whole chat into my text file before I trust any summary. So “Copy everything first” should be the blue button, not the grey one.

Q24s3b. What would you check in the carried-over summary?
PR

What’s missing, and I can’t edit what’s missing. Then three things. The meanings, not the counts: “’Booking’ has 3 meanings” is a pointer, not a summary. Which 7 statuses are matched? “7 of 23” is useless when I’m checking against Dana’s sheet. And every open question still has its label, “unconfirmed” especially, because that’s the word summaries drop first. Actually, the thing that should carry over is the board from the other concept: terms, questions, events. Carry the board, not a paragraph about it.

Q25s3 says “Nothing here is deleted until you choose.” After the reload in s4, only the summary is there.
PR

So when I pressed “Start fresh,” that was me choosing? Then the old conversation is gone, and I didn’t know that was the choice. Keep it readable somewhere, even read-only. The draft surviving is good, though. I’ve lost a typed message to an error before, and it’s small things like that that make me trust the rest.

Q26The notice says the conversation is “kept in this browser.” Does that change what you’d paste? Where do you keep sensitive work notes today?

Plain text files in a folder on my work laptop. It syncs to the company OneDrive, because IT forces Documents to sync. Anything half-shareable goes in Confluence drafts, and nothing about work lives on my own laptop except the made-up version from August. “Kept in this browser” doesn’t change what I paste, because that’s decided by where it’s sent. It changes what I’d leave there overnight. And in March IT re-imaged my laptop and Chrome lost everything local. My text files survived because of OneDrive.

Q27So is browser storage good or bad for you?

It’s the right default for a stranger’s app. I’d rather it sit in my browser than on your server. But there’s a catch nobody’s mentioned. If my policy means I use this at home with sanitized text, the board lives on my home laptop and the RFC lives on my work laptop. So I’ll copy it out anyway. That’s why the table export matters more to me than the storage.

Roadmap hotspots

Assumptions this interview challenges

Rich-text export can follow paste and persistence

#6 at P2 · #4 · #5 at P1

For the RFC due 30 October, the table is the one thing she'd use. It's the only interview-02 export ask that 01b still misses.

Summarising is the way past the conversation cap

B38 #7

A Claude summary merged REBOOKED/AMENDED and dropped TONU. Carry the structured board, make copying out the default, and keep the old conversation readable.

Browser-only persistence answers “don't lose my stuff”

#5

It covers a reload. It doesn't cover policy pushing her to a home machine while the RFC lives at work, or an IT re-image. #5 needs #6 as its backup.

Anonymising at paste time eases the policy problem

#4 slice 3 · #34

It removes colleague names only. Customers, rates, lanes, contract terms and load IDs remain, and the vendor block is untouched. Only a provider path (#34) moves her to real material.

The data-flow notice is settled

#2 (closed)

The two concepts show two notices. Neither lists contract terms, and the draft in 02's own demo pastes one. Revisit the wording in 03b against the live app.

Attributing disagreement by team is safe

B05 #10

It's safe for exports, but it hides disagreement inside a team (Dana and Maya, both ops). Once Dana is away, “Ops” silently means Maya's version.

A cut marker is enough

#44 · B35 #8

It has to sit at the cut and stay visible, and Continue has to append. The better fix is a reply shape that fits.

Voice has a debrief job

slice 12 · #27

Her one real try was never played back. Dictation is unproven, and it would need its own vendor answer.
