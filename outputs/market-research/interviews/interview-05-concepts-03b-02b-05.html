The right columns make the table pasteable

#6

The columns pass now. The cells and the clipboard don't: relative dates, stale checks, a bracketed split, and no date line or open questions on the clipboard. Acceptance should test cell freshness and clipboard contents, not headers.

A Source records where a line came from

#4 · slice 3

It copied “Tue” from the thread and gave the Code row a file name when no code was pasted. Sources need absolute dates or a blank, plus “from code you pasted” or “Guess:”. Yes/no lines must not re-ask checked rows.

02b now carries the board without loss

#7 · B38

“Nothing missing” passes. “Nothing stale” is untested because the fixture edited her note. The “word for word” copy still contradicts her test.

Disagreement is between teams

#10 · B05

The live split is inside ops, a practice against a document. Default team-level swaps (“Maya → Ops”) erase it.

An Example Map is the thing to ship

#19 · B22

The name “Example Mapping” reconnected her to a workshop that didn't stick. What she kept was the move inside a real question. Consider shipping B22 as a move in the questions before a separate map view.

Citations can be optional

#58

She can't check a citation she can't see. Technique sources are citations too, and the hotspot paraphrase failed her first check.

Knowledge-base sources are only an Evans question

Exploration 05 cites a Cucumber guide, Brandolini's book and Collaborative Software Design. If they aren't in the corpus, the coach is citing from memory, the exact phantom-citation risk #58 exists to stop. Either #59 admits them, or our own notes define the moves and the names go.
