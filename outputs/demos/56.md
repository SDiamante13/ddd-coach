## Findings (fresh-eyes pass)

1. **With "Your swaps" open, the sticky composer covers the log (P2, UX).** At 1280×577 the composer card with 5 chips spans roughly y 93–548, about 80% of the viewport. After a send the "You" bubble and most of the reply sit behind it (video 1:17–1:32 and 1:50). The "New reply ↓" pill shows but can't reveal the reply, because the panel is still in the way. A visitor who keeps the panel open to check her list can't read the answer without closing it. Options: close the panel on send, or move "Your swaps" out of the sticky composer. #66's "alert above the composer" geometry doesn't hold with the panel open.
2. **Replies aren't restored (AC 9, D4 cut line).** The coach answers in "Customer A" and "Lane 1". That's expected for this build, and the panel doesn't promise it, but with a longer list she has to map placeholders back by eye. It stays a candidate follow-up.
3. **Role text next to a swapped name still leaves, and the coach uses it.** "Maya (night desk)" became "Person 1 (night desk)", and reply 1 named that group "Ops (night desk)". The note says only listed words are hidden, so this is by design. It just shows that team labels in parentheses carry identity the swap doesn't touch.
4. **Tooling:**
   - `record start` opens a context **without** the previous context's localStorage, despite `record --help` saying it preserves it. Take 1's list was gone at take 2's start, and a list seeded in the base context didn't carry over either. The cookie did carry. Persistence therefore has to be shown with an in-take reload.
   - `eval "location.reload()"` mid-recording kept capturing the first time (2:11) but **stopped the recorder silently the second time**. The "still (0) after reload" step was checked by `eval` only.
   - The "What's sent" region has no visible heading (only `aria-label`), and with the panel open it renders below the panel, far from its toggle. That's minor, but on video it isn't obvious which block is the preview until the marks show.
