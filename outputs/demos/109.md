## Findings

1. **At readable zoom, most of the Words lane can't be reached.** At 100% the lane's first card spans y 558–1267 on a board that ends at 786. The row-locked pan (`translateExtent` = the lane's height) doesn't move vertically: a drag up and a wheel down both leave `translate(8px, 16px)`. The only way to read the lower rows is to zoom out (Fit is 53%, where the row text is about 7 px). After the stub reply, the grown "late" card also runs under the zoom controls at the restored 53%.
2. **Source line from another holder.** On "on time", the **Ops (day desk)** row cites "Mon 10:02 **Ops night desk**: nights mark a load late…" as its source line. A holder-blind match is plausible; worth a check against #6's "real source" rule.
