## Findings

1. **▾ moves the conversation (bug, medium).** At 1280×577, after reply 2 with the composer at rest, clicking ▾ open and then closed moves reply 2's entry from y 85 to y 638, below the composer. The reader loses their place. Keyboard focus on the toggle alone scrolls the log 249 px. A likely cause: `scroll-padding-top: var(--pinned-reserve)` covers the pinned box itself, so focusing its own toggle "scrolls it into view" below the reserve, and the reserve then changes as the box grows and shrinks. One fix: `scroll-margin-top: calc(-1 * var(--pinned-reserve))` on the pinned box's focusables, or keep the scroll position on toggle. A browser test is needed; jsdom can't see it.
2. **Expanded question is capped at 32vh (low-medium).** At 577 the expanded box is 185 px against a 394 px card: the label plus half a line of the question show, and the quotes need an inner scroll. The 3 s auto-open shows the same cut, so the highlight shows the roles label and not the question. At 800 the full card is capped at 256 px, and the second quote is cut (this predates #111).
3. **After Send the composer stays expanded (low, by design).** Focus stays in the box, so the band is 73–314 (241 px) until the visitor clicks away. The 426 px band shows only at rest.
4. **Reply 3's chip still reads "← 0 new on the board · 1 already there"** for an updated card (#94 finding 2, still open).
