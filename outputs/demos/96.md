## Gaps and observations

1. **After reply 2 the board pans to the new cards,** so card 1 and most of the arc are off-screen; only the arc's end at card 3 shows. My wheel pan did not bring it back. Survival is proven by the DOM (1 edge, same aria-label), not by a full on-camera arc.
2. **The link handle overlaps a lifted card.** The handle sits at the slot's bottom (y 264), but a selected card grows to 180 px, so its blue handle dot shows over the title text ("becau●se it is on").
3. The note popover of card 4 reaches the board's right edge (887). Its right border is clipped by the board.
4. The "Board changes" live region keeps its last text ("Already linked.") after reply 2 and after Clear. Screen readers announce only on change, so this is cosmetic, but a later identical message would not be re-announced.
5. The drag was scripted with Playwright's mouse (down, 30 moves, up) on the handle; no real trackpad. Handles are opacity 0 until hover.
