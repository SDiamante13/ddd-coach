# Demo: the board is the page; events become cards (#94, roadmap 4a)

**This is a local fixture demo.** The hosted deploy is verified separately (see `94-deploy.md`). There were **no paid calls**, and no password or `.env` was used.

Recorded 2026-09-26 from a pinned, detached worktree of `main` at `681a1d1`, outside the repo (`npm ci`, then `npm run demo:board` on http://localhost:5190). The worktree was removed afterwards. The fixture answers `GET /api/session` with 204 (which resets its counter on every page load) and each `POST /api/chat` with the next of the 3 canned replies in `src/test/boardDemoReplies.ts`:
1. the real v11 reply to "Try an example thread";
2. a hand-written v11-shaped reply: 1 repeat, 1 new From-thread event, 1 Guess;
3. a hand-written reply that restates reply 2's guess as From thread.

**Tooling:** the recording used Playwright (`playwright-core` with agent-browser's Chrome 147, `recordVideo` at 1280×800) and not agent-browser. On this machine agent-browser 0.23.0's `screenshot` and `record` hung, even on example.com (finding 6).

## Findings

1. **UPDATED is invisible when a card changes in consecutive replies (bug, medium).** Reply 2 adds the guess (JUST ADDED fades at 3 s). Reply 3 restates it: the `li` gets `data-change="updated"` and the tag text becomes "UPDATED", but the computed tag `visibility` is `hidden` and the outline is transparent from +0.3 s. The cause: the `li`, the button and the `.card-tag` span are reused (stable DOM, by design), and `[data-change] > .card` matches throughout (added → updated), so `settle` and `settle-ring` (`board.css` 116–126) have already finished and never restart. jsdom can't see this. One fix: remount the mark per change, for example by keying the tag and a ring wrapper on the change and `board.latest`. This is exactly the guess-becomes-agreement moment the slice exists to show.
2. **The reply-3 chip reads "← 0 new on the board · 1 already there" for an updated card (low, copy).** The card changed, so "already there" understates it. Consider "· 1 updated".
3. **At 390 px the board isn't at the top (low).** It sits after the purpose line and the data-flow notice (y 505), and the sticky composer covers the lower half of the cards at first view. Plan B-D1 says "the board sits in flow at the top". There's no horizontal scroll.
4. **Laptop height is cramped (known, #111).** At 1280×577 the conversation has about 159 px between the pinned question and the composer.
5. **No "old label struck through" on UPDATED.** Per plan B-D3 this arrives with #95's `renameCard`, so it isn't counted against 4a.
6. **Tooling:**
   - agent-browser 0.23.0's `screenshot` hung (even with `--headed`, software GL, or a fresh session on example.com), and `record stop` failed with an ffmpeg error because no frames were captured.
   - Playwright MCP was locked by another session.
   - The fallback, `playwright-core` driving agent-browser's Chrome with `recordVideo` and `page.screenshot`, worked first time. It needs `npx playwright-core install ffmpeg` once.
   - Video time ≈ script time + 0.4 s.
