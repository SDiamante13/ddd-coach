The board merges them into one term with 3 rows ("Words · 1 term · 3 rows"), and the later lowercase meanings replace the first ones (Ops: "A promise to the customer, even if the carrier has done something else."). The margin chip says **"2 terms on the board · 5 rows"**.
2. **The Carrier desk row stays unsourced after who's who.** Rosa Delgado (put on Carrier desk) wrote "for carriers it's contractual. once they've accepted, cancelling costs us a TONU if the truck's rolling", which matches the Carrier desk meaning, but the row still says "No line in your paste matches closely." That's the "1 still none".
3. **Header separator missing:** the counts and the source count render as "…· 3 rows2 with a source line", with no " · " between them, and the line wraps at 1280.
