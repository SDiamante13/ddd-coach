## Findings

1. **Esc with the menu open and focus on its button.** Enter opens the menu but leaves focus on "I checked ▾"; Esc there doesn't close the menu and moves focus to "Zoom out" (the board handler). Esc only works once focus is inside the menu (e.g. Tab to "Where?").
2. **Settle by wraps at 1280 px.** The counts line ends at x 571, and the ~385 px "Settle by: Finance review, 27 Oct 2026 · typed by you" doesn't fit before the band's edge (887), so it takes its own line and the canvas loses 11 px.
