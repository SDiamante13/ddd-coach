## Gaps

1. **"updated" chip part not shown.** Reply 3's restatement hits the corrected card, and `board.ts` (the `correctedFrom` guard on restatement) keeps the visitor's wording, so the card is not marked UPDATED. The chip therefore reads "← 1 already on the board", and the seen chips have at most two parts ("← 2 new on the board · 1 already there"). An "updated" part only appears for an uncorrected restated card; it is covered by `eventBoard.test.tsx` ("counts a card the reply updated as updated…"), not this take. It's a product question whether a restatement of a corrected card should say "already there".
2. Esc was shown only on the keyboard path, not after a double-click.
3. The request body was not inspected, so there's no on-camera proof that, with the flag off, no corrections field is sent.
4. Keyboard focus on ▾ used `focus()`, not a Tab walk.
5. The paste-to-wake path of the resting composer was not shown (keystroke only).
6. At 800 (the still), the pinned card shows the full question and first quote. The second quote needs the inner scroll, as accepted.
7. Not in this issue's scope: dragging a sticky (position by entityId) isn't built or shown.
