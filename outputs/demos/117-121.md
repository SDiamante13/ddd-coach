## Gaps and observations

1. **Selecting a card grows the whole row** (119 → 180 px for all 5), because the row keeps one height. That matches the spec, but the Then arrows and the other cards jump by 61 px on each select and deselect. Design may want the selected title to overflow instead.
2. **The corrected card's own title is clamped too.** "Ops asks the customer before booking another carrie…" loses the visitor's last word until the card is selected. The struck old words show in full below it.
3. The fade is a mask; I didn't pixel-check it. Quote 2's first line sits in the 24 px fade zone (312 vs 309–333), so it reads as a faded partial line, not a clean whole-quote end. The issue allowed either option.
4. Clamp and one row height were checked on reply 1's 5 cards and at 1280×800 only. Not checked: 1280×577, 390 px, or a row with the GUESS card selected.
5. I didn't check that the export keeps full titles ("never lost; the export keeps it whole"), and didn't look at the source popover.
6. The request body was not inspected, so there's no proof that no corrections field is sent with the flag off.
7. Chrome 154, not 153: the system Chrome auto-updated. The agent-team.md rule is 153+.
