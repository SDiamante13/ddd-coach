## Gaps and observations

1. **The held reply shows "1 updated ▸", not "N new ▸".** The fixture's reply 3 only updates; no reply adds new cards off-screen while Follow is off. The new-cards wording ("N new ▸") is covered by tests and by the #124 demo, not this take.
2. **The "2 later ▸" edge chip covers the selected card's text** at the right edge after the #126 pan (about 0:40: "percent… 2 later ▸ … review").
3. **The first-focus hint shows only once per page load.** Any earlier card click uses it up, so the take reloads before the keyboard walk.
4. Tab order: from the heading it took 4 Tabs to reach the first card (through the board controls or other focusables first). I didn't map the exact order.
5. **Fixture replay:** the hotspot "kept across a reply" uses the fixture's reply 1 re-asking the same question after a reload. A reply with a different question creates a new hotspot, so its position starts fresh (by design, keyed by the question).
6. Drags are synthetic mouse input. Trackpad pinch, touch, and keyboard zoom weren't tested.
7. Not checked: reduced motion (instant glide), 577 and 390 px, a session saved before Follow coach existed (cf92135), screen-reader output of the hint beyond `aria-describedby`, and the MiniMap's pannable drag.
