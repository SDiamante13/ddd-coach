## Gaps and observations

1. **Reload replays the "new" marks.** After the restore reload, the restored cards show **JUST ADDED** again, and both restored log lines (correction and link) show the 3 s blue ring again, as if they had just happened. The link also stays "Just drawn" until the next turn. This may be intended for Just drawn, but JUST ADDED and the ring on restored content read as new activity.
2. Only the long-wording case of #123 was shown ("was: …"). The short case (her title fits, old words on one struck line with an ellipsis) is not on camera.
3. The link handle dot shows over the corrected card's "was: …" line while the pointer rests on the card (the same handle-overlap family as the #96 note).
4. Retry's answer is canned reply 1 (fixture counter reset), so it restates reply 1's events. It proves the retry path, not realistic content.
5. Not checked: a stored entry with another version, or valid JSON holding an invalid exchange or action (the code rejects both; only "{bad" was shown). Also not checked: storage full or blocked, swaps surviving New conversation, reduced motion for the ring.
6. The board pans to the newest cards, so after the restore only 2 of 7 cards are on screen. The count comes from the DOM.
