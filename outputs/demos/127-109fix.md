## Findings

1. **The board takes two Tab stops.** `div.react-flow__nodes` ("Events on the board", `tabindex=0`) is focusable before the roving card, so Tab from the notice links lands on the list container first and on a card only with the second Tab. Tabbing out of a card lands on the **React Flow attribution** link before the zoom controls.
2. **The hint is stored as seen only when focus leaves the board.** After Esc it sits on "Zoom out", which is inside the board, so the hint stays up. A reload at that point (before leaving) would show it again. The once-per-browser path works once focus moves elsewhere (here, the message box).
3. **The words-only header reads "Events 0 events · Words · 1 term · 3 rows".** The empty events part could be dropped.
4. **The "late" row's dotted line runs under the neighbouring "weekly late report" card** on its way to the question (visible in the still). The "on time" line starts at its right edge and stays clear.
5. At 100% the zoom controls, the Follow switch and the MiniMap sit over the lower Words lane rows (bottom-left and bottom-right of the board). The keys hint bar also covers the controls while shown.
