## Gaps and observations

1. **Only the 1-event case is on camera.** Both canned questions have one quote that maps to an event card and one that doesn't ("in the contract a load is late…" is an Account team line with no card), so every hotspot shows 1 dotted line and "linked to 1 event". The 2-event case ("relates to Event 1 and Event 2") is covered by `questionHotspot.test.tsx`, not this take.
2. **The hotspot sits under the event it joins, not at the end of the row.** It's placed at the average x of its linked events, 96 px below the row. "After the events" holds for DOM and reading order (last listitem), not for left-to-right position. With 1 link, it sits directly under Event 4 or Event 2.
3. The swap case relies on a route that rewrites the canned reply into swapped space. It shows the client matching in swapped space and restoring names, not a real model honouring the swap.
4. The "On the board ↖ …" chip sits just under the pinned card and slightly overlaps the top of the log entry beneath it in the 800 px margin (see `97.png`).
5. Not checked: zoomed-out boards, keyboard activation of the chip (only clicked), screen-reader output beyond the aria-label, 577 and 390 px widths, and a restore reload with a hotspot.
