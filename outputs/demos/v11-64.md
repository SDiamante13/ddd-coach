## Findings (fresh-eyes pass)

1. **The model merged the markup into one line (low, test strength).** Asked for three lines, it answered `**bold** / <img src=x onerror=alert(1)> / # heading` on a single line, so `# heading` wasn't at the start of a line. The hosted check proves no `strong`, `img` or `h1` for this input, but it didn't exercise a line-start `#`. The acceptance tests cover the line-start case with a stubbed reply.
2. **On a short phone viewport the card only just fits (low, UX).** At 390×578 the card is 307 px tall and the sticky composer starts at y 361, which leaves about 317 px under a caption. The whole card fits only when scrolled precisely (1:13). At a real 390×844 there's plenty of room. Nothing to fix now, but a long question plus long quotes would overflow the gap on a small phone, and the composer would cover the source lines.
3. **Source quotes can carry the thread's own straight quotes.** F2 run 1 quoted `dashboard "bookings today" counts REBOOKED rows twice. who owns the fix?`. The parser keeps it whole, and the card would render `“dashboard "bookings today" … fix?”`. That's readable, just mixed quote styles. The quotes are also sometimes lower-case fragments ("issued Customer D…", "finance only needs…"), which is correct for verbatim excerpts.
4. **Tooling:**
   - `set viewport` mid-recording does resize the page (`innerWidth` 390), but the recorder **stretches** the page into its fixed 1280×578 frame instead of cropping. The 390 and 800-tall segments had to be scaled back to their true shape in ffmpeg (`scale=390:578,pad=1280:578:445:0` and `scale=925:578,pad=…`). Setting the height to 578 keeps the 390 segment 1:1 on the vertical axis.
   - One `set viewport 1280 577` after the PNG silently didn't apply (its output was discarded), so step 4's send was recorded at 1280×800 and needed the second rescale. Check `innerWidth`/`innerHeight` after every `set viewport`.
   - A 1280×800 `screenshot` taken **inside** the recording worked and matched the live page, which saved a paid call (no re-unlock after `record stop`). The squashed stretch was cut from the video.
