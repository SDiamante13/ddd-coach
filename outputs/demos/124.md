## Findings

1. **The note is still clipped for a card that runs past the board edge.** Card 5 (right 980, board right 887) gets `data-align="end"`, but the note aligns to the card's own right edge, so 93 px stay hidden. Clamp the note to the board's visible edge (or pan the card into view on select).
2. **The reply's three-part chip overflows the margin column.** "← 1 new on the board · 1 updated · 1 already there" is clipped at the right edge (visible at about 0:56). In `124.png` its end peeks out beside the pinned question card.
3. After the restore reload at zoom 0.57, the links are off-screen (only 2 of 8 cards in view), so "links in ink" after the reload is proven by the DOM, not by an on-camera arc.

## Flaky reading (honest note)

In one earlier run, the stacked-links readout right after the keyboard link and Esc returned **0 link edges**. The edges were re-rendering, and both were present 5 s later (runs 166/178). The first full run read both links (166/178) at the same step with no wait. The final take waits for 2 link edges before reading. I treat it as a measurement race in my script, not an app bug, but it's unconfirmed.
